Installation Guides

Jump to: Engine Oil | Battery | Brake Pads | Air Filter | Wipers | Headlights

How to Change Engine Oil

Difficulty: Easy • Time: 30–45 min • Tools: socket wrench, drain pan, oil filter wrench, funnel, jack stands

⚠ Safety: Never work under a car supported only by a jack. Let the engine cool first; oil is hot.

  1. Warm the engine for 2 minutes, then park on level ground and turn it off.
  2. Raise the vehicle and secure it on jack stands.
  3. Place the drain pan and remove the drain plug. Let the oil drain fully.
  4. Replace the filter after oiling the new gasket. Reinstall the drain plug.
  5. Add new oil, check the dipstick, run the engine and check for leaks.

How to Replace a Car Battery

Difficulty: Easy • Time: 20–30 min • Tools: 10mm wrench, terminal cleaner, gloves, eye protection

⚠ Safety: Wear eye protection. Disconnect negative first and reconnect it last to avoid sparks.

  1. Turn off the vehicle and remove the key.
  2. Disconnect the negative (−) cable, then the positive (+).
  3. Remove the hold-down bracket and lift out the old battery.
  4. Clean the tray and terminals, then set the new battery in place.
  5. Reconnect positive first, then negative, and tighten firmly.

How to Replace Brake Pads

Difficulty: Moderate • Time: 1–2 hours • Tools: jack, jack stands, lug wrench, C-clamp, socket set, brake grease

⚠ Safety: Brakes are safety-critical. Never inhale brake dust and test brakes before driving.

  1. Loosen lug nuts, lift the vehicle and remove the wheel.
  2. Remove the caliper bolts and hang the caliper with wire.
  3. Take out the old pads and clean the bracket.
  4. Compress the piston with a C-clamp, apply grease and fit the new pads.
  5. Reinstall the caliper and wheel, then pump the brake pedal before driving.

How to Replace an Engine Air Filter

Difficulty: Easy • Time: 5–10 min • Tools: screwdriver (if clips are not tool-free)

⚠ Safety: Make sure the engine is off and cool before opening the airbox.

  1. Locate the airbox, a black plastic case near the engine.
  2. Release the clips or screws and lift the cover.
  3. Remove the old filter and wipe out debris.
  4. Insert the new filter with the rubber rim seated.
  5. Close the cover and secure all clips.

How to Replace Windshield Wipers

Difficulty: Easy • Time: 5 min • Tools: none

⚠ Safety: Fold a towel under the arm so it cannot snap back and crack the windshield.

  1. Lift the wiper arm away from the glass.
  2. Press the release tab and slide the old blade off the hook.
  3. Slide the new blade on until it clicks.
  4. Lower the arm gently and repeat for the other side.

How to Replace Headlights

Difficulty: Moderate • Time: 20–40 min • Tools: gloves, screwdriver, trim tool

⚠ Safety: Disconnect the battery, let bulbs cool, and never touch new bulb glass with bare fingers.

  1. Open the hood and find the back of the headlight assembly.
  2. Unplug the connector and twist the old bulb out.
  3. Insert the new bulb or LED kit, wearing gloves.
  4. Reconnect the plug, then test low and high beams.
  5. Check aim against a wall at night.
